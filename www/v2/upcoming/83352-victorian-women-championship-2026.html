♟️ Victorian Women’s Championship 2026

Hobsons Bay Chess Club, in partnership with Geelong Chess Club, is proud to host the Victorian Women’s Championship 2026 — a premier classical event celebrating female chess talent across Victoria.

Under the Patronage of Chess Victoria (FIDE & ACF Rated Classical Event)

📅 Dates:

    • Saturday 12 September 2026
    • Saturday 19 September 2026

📍 Venue:

    • Hobsons Bay Chess Club, 108 Queen St, Altona VIC

🏆 Event Details

    • Format: 5 Round Swiss
    • Time Control: 60 minutes + 30 seconds increment (from move 1)
    • Rated: FIDE Classical & ACF Standard Rated
    • Tie-breaks: Buchholz Cut 1 (BH-C1), Buchholz (BH), Sonneborn-Berger (SB)

👩 Sections

    • Open Women’s Championship
    • Junior Girls Section (subject to sufficient entries)
    • Additional sections may be introduced subject to entries and Chess Victoria approval

    • 12:45 – 1:30 Lunch Break
    • 1:30 – 4:30 Round 2

Week 2 – Saturday 19 September 2026

💰 Prizes

    • Total Cash Prize Pool: $1500
    • Trophies for Top 3 Overall (official title)
    • Best Age Group (number of age groups depends on players registered)
    • Best Unrated (Unofficial title)
    • Best Upset (both players must be rated)
    • Best Geelong Chess Club/ Hobsons Bay Chess Club player
