3:T2fd6,<h1><strong>♟️ HBCC JETS 2026 &ndash; Junior Elite Training Squad</strong></h1>

<p><strong>Developing Tomorrow&#39;s Chess Masters!</strong></p>

<p>Hobsons Bay Chess Club (HBCC) is proud to introduce <strong>JETS 2026 &ndash; Junior Elite Training Squad</strong>, a high-performance chess development initiative designed to nurture promising junior players and provide a structured pathway towards achieving internationally recognised chess titles.</p>

<p>JETS brings together <strong>16 selected junior players from Hobsons Bay Chess Club and Geelong Chess Club</strong> for an intensive three-day program combining master-level coaching, advanced chess training, competitive play, mentorship and team bonding.</p>

<p>Our ambition is to develop the next generation of Victorian and Australian chess champions, national representatives and future chess masters.</p>

<p><strong>More than a squad. A pathway to masters.</strong></p>

<hr />
<h2><strong>📅 Program Details</strong></h2>

<p><strong>Dates:</strong> Friday 27 &ndash; Sunday 29 November 2026<br />
<strong>Duration:</strong> 3 days, 2 nights<br />
<strong>Venue:</strong> Urban Camp, 7 Brens Drive, Parkville VIC 3052 (proposed)<br />
<strong>Participants:</strong> 16 selected juniors (14 HBCC and 2 Geelong Chess Club)<br />
<strong>Training Level:</strong> Approximately FIDE 1700&ndash;2200<br />
<strong>Program Coordinator:</strong> IA Casey Goh</p>

<p>&nbsp;</p>

<h3>For HBCC Juniors</h3>

<p>HBCC will contribute <strong>$200 per selected junior</strong>, covering approximately 44% of the program cost.</p>

<p>With 14 HBCC juniors participating, this represents a total club investment of <strong>$2,800</strong> towards junior development.</p>

<h3>For Geelong Chess Club Juniors</h3>

<p>The two participating Geelong Chess Club juniors may approach Geelong Chess Club individually to seek a subsidy towards their participation costs.</p>

<p>Any financial assistance will be subject to Geelong Chess Club&#39;s own approval and arrangements. The HBCC subsidy does not automatically apply to Geelong Chess Club participants.</p>

<p>As a community-based, volunteer-run chess club, HBCC believes in investing directly in junior development and creating opportunities that extend beyond regular club coaching and tournaments.</p>

<p>&nbsp;</p>

<h2><strong>🎯 What is JETS?</strong></h2>

<p>JETS aims to accelerate junior chess development through structured, high-level training and practical competitive experience.</p>

<p>The program focuses on:</p>

<ul>
	<li>
	<p><strong>Master-Level Coaching:</strong> Advanced chess lectures and mentorship from experienced titled players.</p>
	</li>
	<li>
	<p><strong>Competitive Development:</strong> Chess puzzles, simultaneous exhibitions, practical challenges and blitz competition.</p>
	</li>
	<li>
	<p><strong>Mental Strength:</strong> Building confidence, discipline, resilience and a competitive mindset.</p>
	</li>
	<li>
	<p><strong>Team Bonding:</strong> Strengthening friendships and encouraging juniors to learn from one another.</p>
	</li>
	<li>
	<p><strong>International Preparation:</strong> Preparing players for major national and international tournaments.</p>
	</li>
	<li>
	<p>&nbsp;</p>
	</li>
</ul>

<p><strong>Selection Committee:</strong> Anirudh Krishnan and Casey Goh.</p>
