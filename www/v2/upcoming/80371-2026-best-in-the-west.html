2026 Best in the West

Saturday 29 August 2026 – Sunday 30 August 2026Finnish Hall
Registration

The flagship chess weekend in Melbourne’s Western Suburbs returns!

The Hobsons Bay Best in the West is back for 2026 — a premier classical weekend tournament attracting strong players from across Victoria and beyond.

📅 Dates

Saturday 29 August – Sunday 30 August 2026

📍 Venue

Finnish Hall, 119 Pier St, Altona

 

🏆 Tournament Details

  • Format: 5-Round Swiss (no tiebreaks)

  • Pairing: We will be using Baku accelerated pairing if we exceed 100 players. 

  • Time Control: 60 minutes + 30 seconds increment per move
    (Scoresheet required)
  • Rating: FIDE & ACF Rated
  • Maximum Entries: 120 players
  • Recommended Rating: ≥ 1000 ACF or FIDE

 Schedule

Saturday – 29 August 2026

  • Registration & Player Briefing: 9:00 AM – 9:30 AM
  • Round 1: 9:30 AM
  • Lunch Break: 12:30 PM – 1:30 PM
  • Round 2: 1:30 PM
  • Round 3: 5:30 PM

Sunday – 30 August 2026

  • Round 4: 9:30 AM
  • Lunch Break: 12:30 PM – 1:30 PM
  • Round 5: 1:30 PM

💰 Entry Fees

  • Early Bird: $80 (Entries must be received by 14th August 2026)

  • Early Bird Concession (Pensioners & Juniors): $60 (Entries must be received by 14th August 2026)
  • Free Entry for GM / WGM / IM / WIM / FM / WFM / CM / WCM (Entries must be received by 14th August 2026)
  • Late Entry Fee: +$15 for entries received after Friday 14th August 2026

 

🏅 Prize Pool –Guaranteed

  • 1st Place: $1,200 (Guaranteed)

  • 2nd Place: $600
  • 3rd Place: $300

  • Best Juniors (Guaranteed): $1,000 -Proudly sponsored by Bendigo Community Bank, Laverton and Altona - tie breaker will be used to decide the prize

  • Best Ladies: $300 (female may win more than 1 prize, ACF by-law (Open or rating group and female)
  • Rating Group 1 Winner: $200
  • Rating Group 2 Winner: $200
  • Best Unrated Player: $200
  • Biggest Upset: $100

📜 Prize Rules

  • No tiebreaks — prizes are shared equally between players on the same score (except Best Junior $1000 sponsored by Bendigo Community Bank- Laverton and Altona)

  • Players may win one prize only (highest value prize applies). Female players may win more than one prize, as per ACF by-law (Open or rating group and female)
  • Players with an ACF Classical rating will be divided into three equal rating groups:
    • Top group: not eligible for rating prizes
    • Middle group: Rating Group 1
    • Bottom group: Rating Group 2
  • Players without an ACF rating but with a FIDE Classical rating will be placed at the Chief Arbiter’s discretion.
  • Best Unrated prize is only for players without ACF or FIDE ratings.
  • Biggest Upset is awarded for the largest upward rating differential win.
  • Rating groups will be published by Round 2.

➕ Byes & Withdraws

  • One ½-point bye available in Rounds 1–3

  • Round 1 bye must be requested in advance of tournament commencement
  • Any Withdraw/ refund request must be received latest by 25th August 2026. No withdraw/ refund would be entertained after this date.
