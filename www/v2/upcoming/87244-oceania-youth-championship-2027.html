♟️ Oceania Youth Chess Championships 2027

🌏 29 September – 3 October 2027 | Melbourne, Australia

Hobsons Bay Chess Club (HBCC) is proud to host the Oceania Youth Chess Championships 2027, bringing together talented young chess players from across the Oceania region.

We anticipate welcoming 300–400 players from up to 13 Oceania federations for an exciting international chess event in Melbourne's west.

📍 Tournament Information

  • Dates: 29 September – 3 October 2027
  • Venue: Bacchus Marsh Grammar – Woodlea Campus, Victoria, Australia
  • Host: Hobsons Bay Chess Club (HBCC)
  • Eligibility: Junior players up to Under 20 (born in 2007 or later, subject to age-category requirements)
  • Rating: FIDE-rated championships

✅ What Does Registration Cover?

Your registration fee includes:

  • Entry to the Oceania Youth Classical Championship
  • Entry to the Oceania Youth Rapid Championship
  • Entry to the Oceania Youth Blitz Championship
  • Applicable championship registration levy

Accommodation, meals and optional transport are not included in the standard non-official player registration fee. Separate arrangements may apply to eligible official federation players.

🌴 Pacific Island Federation Assistance Scheme

To encourage greater participation from Pacific Island nations, HBCC is offering financial assistance to eligible federations.

  • AUD $1,000 assistance per eligible federation
  • Available to the first three qualifying federations
  • Minimum of five registered players per federation
  • Australia and New Zealand are excluded
  • Additional terms and conditions apply
