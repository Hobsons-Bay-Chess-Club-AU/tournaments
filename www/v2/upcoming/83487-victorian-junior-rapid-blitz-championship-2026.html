♟️ Victorian Junior Rapid & Blitz Championships 2026

📅 Dates

Saturday, 3 October 2026 – State Juniors Rapid Championship
(Note: Registering for Rapid does NOT include Blitz — separate registration required)

 

Sunday, 4 October 2026 – State Juniors Blitz Championship
(Note: Registering for Blitz does NOT include Rapid — separate registration required)

 

📍 Venue

Bacchus Marsh Grammar – Maddingley Campus
37 South Maddingley Rd, Maddingley VIC 3340

 

🚀 Event Highlights

  • 🌟 First time held in Melbourne’s West
  • ⚡ First-ever Victorian Junior Blitz Championship
  • ♟️ FIDE Rated for U12–U18 sections
  • 📡 DGT live boards for featured games
  • 🛍️ Vendor showcase: ChessNut & ChessLife
  • 🏛️ Attendance by Members of Parliament and media coverage

 

🧭 Getting There

 

🚗 By Car (Recommended)

  • ~40 mins from Melbourne CBD via Western Freeway (M8)
  • ~25 mins from Werribee, Point Cook, Tarneit, Caroline Springs
  • ✅ Ample on-site parking with easy drop-off zones
  • ✅ Minimal congestion compared to metro venues

 

🚆 By Public Transport

  • V/Line (Ballarat Line)
  • 🚉 Bacchus Marsh Station (~5 min walk)
  • ~35–45 mins from Southern Cross Station

 

🚸 Junior Drop-off Information

  • Plenty of street parking available
  • Entry via Gate 1, S Maddingley Rd, Maddingley VIC 3340
  • Parents/visitors permitted inside
  • Players will be guided to playing rooms
  • Parents may collect children after each round

⚙️ Tournament Details

  • Rapid: 9 rounds · 13+2
  • Blitz: 11 rounds · 3+2
  • Seeding: ACF Quick → FIDE rating → Arbiter’s discretion

 

Rating

  • U8 & U10: ACF Quick Rated only
  • U12+:
    • Rapid: FIDE Rapid + ACF Quick
    • Blitz: FIDE Blitz + ACF Quick

 

FIDE ID for U12- U18

  • Valid FIDE ID required for FIDE rating.
  • If you have no FIDE ID and
  • you are Non-citizen/non-PR players/ NZ citizens- You must obtain FIDE ID via home federation be eligible to play
  • you are citizen/ PR players- we will register you with Australian Chess Federation
  • If you are not sure, immediately email use at major_tournaments@hobsonsbaychess.com. 

 

🏆 Titles & Divisions

 

Divisions

  • Open: U8, U10, U12, U14, U16, U18
  • Girls: U8, U10, U12, U14, U16, U18

 

Key Notes

  • Girls may play Open OR Girls (not both)
  • Players may play up in higher age groups

 

Additional Prizes

  • 🎖️ Non-title medals (e.g. odd age group categories)
  • 🏅 Up to 72 total prizes
  • 📜 Certificates of participation available

 

📅 Age Eligibility (2026)

  • U18: Born 2008 or later
  • U16: Born 2010 or later
  • U14: Born 2012 or later
  • U12: Born 2014 or later
  • U10: Born 2016 or later
  • U8: Born 2018 or later

 

👨‍👩‍👧 Important Notes

  • U8 & U10 must be accompanied by a parent/guardian
  • Sections may be merged if required (ACF rules)
  • Titles/trophies awarded per ACF regulations
