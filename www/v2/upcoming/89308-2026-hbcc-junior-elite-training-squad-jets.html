♟️ HBCC JETS 2026 – Junior Elite Training Squad

Developing Tomorrow's Chess Masters!

Hobsons Bay Chess Club (HBCC) is proud to introduce JETS 2026 – Junior Elite Training Squad, a high-performance chess development initiative designed to nurture promising junior players and provide a structured pathway towards achieving internationally recognised chess titles.

JETS brings together 16 selected junior players from Hobsons Bay Chess Club and Geelong Chess Club for an intensive three-day program combining master-level coaching, advanced chess training, competitive play, mentorship and team bonding.

Our ambition is to develop the next generation of Victorian and Australian chess champions, national representatives and future chess masters.

More than a squad. A pathway to masters.

📅 Program Details

Dates: Friday 27 – Sunday 29 November 2026
Duration: 3 days, 2 nights
Venue: Urban Camp, 7 Brens Drive, Parkville VIC 3052 (proposed)
Participants: 16 selected juniors (14 HBCC and 2 Geelong Chess Club)
Training Level: Approximately FIDE 1700–2200
Program Coordinator: IA Casey Goh

🎯 What is JETS?

JETS aims to accelerate junior chess development through structured, high-level training and practical competitive experience.

The program focuses on:

  • Master-Level Coaching: Advanced chess lectures and mentorship from experienced titled players.

  • Competitive Development: Chess puzzles, simultaneous exhibitions, practical challenges and blitz competition.

  • Mental Strength: Building confidence, discipline, resilience and a competitive mindset.

  • Team Bonding: Strengthening friendships and encouraging juniors to learn from one another.

  • International Preparation: Preparing players for major national and international tournaments.

👨‍🏫 Meet Our Confirmed Coaches and Speakers

We are delighted to have the following coaches and speakers participating in JETS 2026.

  • IM Haridas Pascua – Endgame Mastery Friday 27 November (Online)

An intensive endgame training session focusing on endgame understanding, practical techniques and improving players' ability to convert advantages and defend difficult positions.

  • FM Grant Szuveges – Middlegame Strategy Saturday 28 November (Face-to-Face)

Advanced middlegame training covering strategic planning, positional understanding and practical decision-making.

  • IM Jairo Hernandez – Simultaneous Chess Exhibition Saturday 28 November (Evening)

An opportunity for our juniors to challenge an International Master in a simultaneous chess exhibition and gain valuable practical experience.

  • FM Rheyansh Annapureddy – Motivational Session Sunday 29 November (Online)

An inspirational session encouraging juniors to pursue their chess ambitions and continue working towards higher levels of competition.

  • Anirudh Krishnan – Chess Puzzles and Trivia Friday 27 November (Evening)

An interactive puzzle-solving challenge and chess trivia session designed to develop calculation, problem-solving and friendly competition.

🏆 Pathway to Chess Masters

JETS is part of HBCC's long-term commitment to developing a sustainable high-performance chess pathway.

Club Player → Competitive Player → Candidate Master (CM) → FIDE Master (FM) → International Master (IM) → Grandmaster (GM)

Through advanced training, mentorship and competitive experience, we aim to prepare juniors for major events such as:

  • Victorian Junior Chess Championships

  • Australian Junior Chess Championships

  • Oceania Youth Chess Championships

  • FIDE Zone 3.6 Zonal Championships

  • Other major FIDE-rated competitions

Beyond chess titles, we also hope to develop future coaches, arbiters and mentors who will contribute to the wider chess community.

♟️ Player Selection

Selection for JETS 2026 was highly competitive, with limited places available.
